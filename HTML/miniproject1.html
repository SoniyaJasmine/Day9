Chocolate Cupcakes

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups granliated sugar
  • 2 cups all purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 1.5 taespoons baking powder
  • 1.5 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1.5 teaspoons vanila extract
  • 3/4 cup boiling water

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 180 degree c.
  2. In a mixing bowl stir sugar, flour, cocoa powder, baking soad, baking powder and salt together until there are no clumps
  3. Add eggs, milk, oil and venilla extract. Beat with a mixers on medium speed for two minutes.
  4. Stir in boiling water
  5. Spoon batter evenly into cupcake wells. Bake for 20 to 22 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
  6. Remove from oven and let cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then remove from pan
  7. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for upto three days.
